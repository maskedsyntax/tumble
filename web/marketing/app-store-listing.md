# Tumble 3.1 App Store listing

The canonical, ASC-validated values live in `metadata/`. This document records
the positioning and screenshot strategy for product review.

## Positioning

**Choose a photo → Find its film character → Refine → Finish as Photo or Postcard**

Tumble is an editing-first vintage photo studio, not a camera constraint or a
small filter utility. Camera capture remains available as a neutral secondary
source. Postcards are the signature finish; clean photo export is equally
valid.

## Search fields

- Name: `Tumble: Vintage Photo Editor`
- Subtitle: `Retro Film Camera & Filters`
- Keywords: `y2k,digicam,cam,analog,grain,nostalgia,90s,old,aesthetic,digital,darkroom,postcard,effects,picture`

The keywords are 98 of 100 characters and repeat no word from the name or
subtitle. They avoid commercial film-stock or camera-brand names and
exact-emulation claims.

"Camera" stays in the subtitle rather than the keyword field because the
3.0 listing's best ranks (vintage film camera, film photo, vintage camera)
depend on it, per Astro on 2026-10-20. Postcard searches sit at Apple's
popularity floor, so "postcard" lives in the keywords and the postcard
finish is sold by screenshot 4 instead of the subtitle.
