# Tumble App Store screenshots — v4

Seven search-first screenshots built from the competitor audit. The first
three sell the result, the range, and Tumble's unique develop ritual; the rest
move through intention, collection, keepsakes, and privacy.

Upload the numbered 6.9-inch PNGs at this folder's root. The renderer also
writes a 6.5-inch fallback set to `6.5-inch/`.

| # | Hook | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real film looks. Straight from camera. | Lead with the desired result. |
| 2 | One shot. Twenty-one looks. | Prove range with large, legible samples. |
| 3 | Shake it. Watch it develop. | Make the unique reveal feel kinetic and rewarding. |
| 4 | Twelve shots. Make them count. | Turn the daily limit into a physical roll of film. |
| 5 | Your days, kept. Never posted. | Place the private archive inside an open drawer. |
| 6 | Four ways to send the moment. | Show every real postcard treatment. |
| 7 | Your photos. On your phone. | Explain local photo storage without obscuring anonymous analytics. |

The v4 set shares the proven rendering engine in `mockups-appstore-v3/main.swift`
and still compiles against the shipping film-stock catalog and grade pipeline.
All photography comes from the licensed archive documented in
`mockups-appstore/PHOTO-LICENSES.md`.

```sh
chmod +x mockups-appstore-v4/render.sh
./mockups-appstore-v4/render.sh
```
