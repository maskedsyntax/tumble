## Full description

Tumble is a private film camera and Studio for shaping photos without sending
them to a photo cloud.

Capture something new or import a photo, swipe through 21 film-inspired looks,
then refine the result in Studio. Compare the original and film versions,
control intensity, rotate and crop, or finish it with one of four postcard
frames and a handwritten note. Save or share only when it feels right.

Six Core looks are free. Tumble Complete is a single $4.99 purchase that unlocks
the fifteen premium looks in Ninety-Six, Darkroom and Long Summer. It is not a
subscription.

The camera, import, editing, saving and sharing never require an account.
Optional Apple or Google sign-in lets you link film purchases across iOS and
Android. Apple and Google process payments; Firebase handles sign-in and
RevenueCat receives an opaque account ID and purchase history.

Your photo pixels, filenames, drafts, crop contents and handwritten notes stay
on your device. Optional anonymous product analytics and diagnostics remain off
until you choose to enable them in Settings.
