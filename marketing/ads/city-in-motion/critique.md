## Slideshow test

Pass. Every still has virtual-camera travel, a moving grade boundary, animated light streaks, and a distinct entrance direction. Street-level and aerial compositions vary materially.

## AI ad test

Pass with one fix: no fake UI, floating cards, generic “elevate” language, or repeated spring animation. The stacked end-card strips created accidental empty space and were corrected to a horizontal contact sheet.

## Product clarity

A muted viewer can identify a before/after film-filter camera app. Revision adds “TUMBLE / DEVELOP” within the transformation sequence instead of waiting until the end card.

## Emotional strategy

Transformation happens five times and remains the main visual event. Movement intensifies the reveal instead of replacing it.

## Claims

“21 FILM LOOKS,” “iPhone film camera,” named filter looks, and App Store availability are verified. The hook is expressive copy, not a performance claim.

## Five highest-impact fixes

1. Make the end-card contact sheet horizontal so all five looks fill the frame.
2. Introduce Tumble during the demonstration, not only after it.
3. Bring the App Store CTA in earlier and hold it longer.
4. Lift the end-card strip brightness so the five-look proof survives the overlay.
5. Preserve the moving background under the end card so the video never drops into a static brand slate.

## Filter-choice revision

User feedback identified the first three grades as the weak point. The original set was visually re-audited against alternate real renders.

- [x] Pool Party → Warm Archive on the neon crossing: more distinct from the clean source and less predictably blue/magenta.
- [x] Overcast → Everyday on the train: restores contrast, keeps the train dominant, and removes the washed veil.
- [x] Light Leak → Pool Party on the rain boulevard: preserves reflections and architectural detail instead of covering them with orange flare.
