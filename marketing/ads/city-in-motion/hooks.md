# Hooks

Each hook must be visually executable in ~1.5s and work with sound off.

## Strategy: transformation

### H1
- Line: THE CITY DOESN'T POSE
- Visual: blurred pedestrians cross diagonally as a luminous grade seam races with them.
- First frame: full-bleed neon crossing with the line dominating the upper third.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 9 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 8 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; expressive headline, not a product-performance claim.

### H2

- Line: KEEP UP WITH THE CITY
- Visual: elevated train tears across frame and pulls the grade behind it.
- First frame: train blur nearly exits the right edge.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 9 / 8 / 9 / 7 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes.

### H3

- Line: CATCH THE BLUR
- Visual: cyclist remains crisp while architecture streaks behind.
- First frame: giant cropped word BLUR behind the rider.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 9 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes.

### H4

- Line: ONE CITY. FIVE ERAS.
- Visual: five angled strips of the same urban world in distinct film color.
- First frame: rapid five-way color segmentation.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 7 / 8 / 8 / 9 / 8 / 7
- Claim-safe: yes as campaign metaphor; the film looks are shown.

### H5

- Line: DON'T FREEZE THE MOMENT
- Visual: rain traffic smears through a frozen photographic frame.
- First frame: static white border crossed by animated light streaks.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 7 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes.
