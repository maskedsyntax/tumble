# Campaign brief

- Product: Tumble
- Campaign: City in Motion
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Format: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, vertical
- Channel: Instagram Reels / short-form social
- Forced strategy (if any): transformation
- Assumptions: the audience understands “before / after”; generated city photos are source material, while every “after” is rendered by Tumble's production-equivalent film-grade pipeline.

## Job of this ad

Stop city photographers and Reels creators with an urban-motion aesthetic, then prove that Tumble can turn clean phone-camera city frames into materially different film looks.

## Single-minded proposition

The city moves fast. Tumble gives each passing frame a film character worth keeping.

## Proof we can show

- Five honest before/after transformations.
- Named, real Tumble looks: Warm Archive, Everyday, Pool Party, Disposable, and Golden Hour.
- “21 film looks,” verified in `marketing/CLAIMS.md`.

Download Tumble on the App Store.
