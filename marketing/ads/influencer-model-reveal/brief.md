# Campaign brief

- Product: Tumble
- Campaign: Influencer Model Reveal
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Format: 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps
- Channel: Instagram Reels / short-form social
- Forced strategy: transformation, matching the prior five-photo reveal format
- Assumptions: original AI-generated editorial portraits are acceptable as campaign source photography; every after image must come from Tumble's production-equivalent Core Image renderer.

## Job of this ad

Stop fashion, lifestyle, and photo-dump creators with an immediately legible edit, then prove that Tumble can give very different portraits distinct film character.

## Single-minded proposition

One source portrait can become a post-ready film look without changing the pose, person, or scene.

## Proof we can show

Five original source photographs paired pixel-for-pixel with five outputs from Tumble's real film-grade pipeline.

## On-screen claims gate

- `EVERYDAY`, `CAMCORDER`, `GOLDEN HOUR`, `NEWSPRINT`, and `WARM ARCHIVE`: real film-stock names in the app.
- `21 FILM LOOKS`: Verified in `marketing/CLAIMS.md`.
- `iPhone film camera`: Verified in `marketing/CLAIMS.md`.
- `DOWNLOAD ON THE APP STORE`: supported by the verified live App Store URL.
- `DON'T POST IT YET`: imperative hook, not a factual claim.
