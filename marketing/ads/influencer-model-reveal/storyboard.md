# Storyboard

Total duration: 15 seconds  
FPS: 30  
Resolution: 1080x1920

## Shot 1 — 0:00-0:02.35
- Purpose: stop fashion creators and prove the edit instantly.
- Camera: macro push from the model's face to a three-quarter print; slight counterclockwise roll.
- Framing / scale / perspective: oversized print fills 85% of frame; face begins near upper third.
- Product position: no device; real image output is the product.
- UI state / action: Everyday grade develops left-to-right over the exact cobalt source portrait.
- Typography: `DON'T POST IT YET`, then `BEFORE` / `EVERYDAY`.
- Transition: the cobalt lapel becomes a blue motion band carrying into shot 2.
- Sound: optional bass hit at the seam; cut remains sound-off legible.
- Emotional intent: confidence and curiosity.
- Slideshow test: moving seam, depth, macro-to-wide camera change, and graphic handoff.
- Copy-removed test: direct gaze and dramatic color change still carry the hook.

## Shot 2 — 0:02.25-0:04.60

- Framing / scale / perspective: full-body portrait with deep vanishing lines and slight clockwise tilt.
- Product position: full-frame print angled in space.
- UI state / action: Camcorder grade wipes bottom-to-top, preserving the same model and pose.
- Typography: `BEFORE` / `CAMCORDER`.
- Transition: steel-wall highlight sweeps into rooftop sky.
- Sound: optional tape click.
- Emotional intent: nocturnal edge.
- Slideshow test: opposite camera direction, different scale, and vertical develop axis.
- Copy-removed test: grain, haze, and darker blue-hour treatment remain legible.

- Copy-removed test: the warmer skin, sky, and garment treatment show the delta.

## Slideshow tests

- Every shot changes crop, camera direction, tilt, develop axis, subject scale, or color grammar.
- The before/after seam is always in motion; no card simply fades between two static screenshots.
- Each frame remains visually useful without copy because the portraits and real grade delta do the selling.
