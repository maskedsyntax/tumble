## Exploring

## Not using, and why

- FOMO/social proof: no adoption evidence, creator endorsements, or engagement data exists.
- Pain-solution: influencer insecurity would feel manipulative and is not necessary when the visual delta is strong.
- Speed: the app's core ritual is intentionally slower, so a speed claim would fight the product.
