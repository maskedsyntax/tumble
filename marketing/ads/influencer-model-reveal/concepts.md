# Concepts

## Concept A — The Editorial Develop
- Strategy: transformation + identity
- Hook: `DON'T POST IT YET`
- Core idea: five diverse fashion portraits develop from neutral digital captures into five distinct Tumble film stocks.
- Visual metaphor: a contact sheet coming alive one print at a time.
- Emotional intent: taste, confidence, creative possibility.
- Product demonstration: exact same-source wipes through real grades.
- Pacing: five 2.25-second portrait beats, then a 3.3-second end card.
- Typography: hook plus before/look labels; no explanatory copy.
- Transitions: print lifts, seam light, match cuts on body lines and color.
- Ending / CTA: final model expands into Tumble brand card and App Store CTA.
- Why it could work: creators see people posing like the content they make, then immediately see a usable aesthetic delta.
- Asset feasibility: complete.
- Execution risk: repetitive cards unless crops and transitions vary.

## Concept B — Pick Your Era
- Strategy: identity
- Hook: `PICK YOUR ERA`
- Core idea: one portrait cycles through several looks before five creators each claim a different visual era.
- Visual metaphor: a casting wall crossed with a film-stock selector.
- Emotional intent: self-expression and taste.
- Product demonstration: fast look switching, less time per before/after.
- Pacing: aggressive first four seconds, then larger held portraits.
- Typography: giant look names partially behind subjects.
- Transitions: match cuts on eyes, shoulders, and negative space.
- Ending / CTA: `21 FILM LOOKS` resolves into Tumble.
- Why it could work: directly frames the product as a creator identity tool.
- Asset feasibility: complete.
- Execution risk: rapid cycling may obscure the honest before state.

## Concept C — The Feed Audition
- Strategy: premium / product-first
- Hook: no line; a neutral portrait is rejected off-frame, then returns as a tactile print.
- Core idea: portraits audition for a place in a highly curated feed.
- Visual metaphor: fashion casting cards sliding across a light table.
- Emotional intent: curation and craft.
- Product demonstration: each accepted card reveals a different Tumble look.
- Pacing: controlled, rhythmic, slightly slower.
- Typography: contact-sheet codes and film-stock names.
- Transitions: physical slide, stamp, light-table flash.
- Ending / CTA: accepted stack becomes the Tumble logo tile.
- Why it could work: premium visual language fits fashion creators.
- Asset feasibility: complete.
- Execution risk: the rejection metaphor could imply Tumble fixes the model rather than the color treatment.

## Ranking

1. Concept A — clearest proof and closest to the successful prior format.
2. Concept B — strongest hook-variant direction.
3. Concept C — stylish but carries the wrong emotional implication.

## Selected

Concept A — The Editorial Develop.

## Shelved for later

Concept B for the next hook-led cut; Concept C only if reframed around the edit, never the person.
