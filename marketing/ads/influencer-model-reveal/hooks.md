# Hooks

Each hook is executable in the first 1.5 seconds and works with sound off.

## Strategy: transformation

### H1 — Don't post it yet
- Line: `DON'T POST IT YET`
- Visual: macro crop on a confident cobalt portrait; a luminous seam immediately replaces the neutral image with the Everyday grade.
- First frame: direct gaze, oversized cobalt tailoring, hook type cropped at the edges.
- Scores (scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty): 9 / 10 / 10 / 9 / 8 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes; imperative, followed by real same-source proof.

### H2 — Pick your era
- Line: `PICK YOUR ERA`
- Visual: rapid match cut between the same eye in Original, Camcorder, and Newsprint.
- First frame: extreme eye crop with an analog timestamp edge entering frame.
- Scores: 9 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 8 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; invitation, not an outcome claim.

### H3 — The edit is the outfit
- Line: `THE EDIT IS THE OUTFIT`
- Visual: green studio portrait loses all color in a fast Newsprint develop sweep.
- First frame: burgundy dress in motion against saturated green.
- Scores: 8 / 10 / 9 / 8 / 9 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; metaphorical line.

### H4 — Five moods, one roll
- Line: `FIVE MOODS. ONE ROLL.`
- Visual: five vertical slivers expand one by one into full portraits.
- First frame: a narrow band of each model aligned like a contact sheet.
- Scores: 8 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 8 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes; the cut visibly contains five looks.

### H5 — Your post, on film
- Line: `YOUR POST, ON FILM`
- Visual: a raw portrait becomes a bordered print while grain and toning develop across it.
- First frame: full-bleed raw face, print edge entering from below.
- Scores: 8 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 8 / 7
- Claim-safe: yes as positioning, but less novel than H1.

## Ranked

1. H1 — Don't post it yet
2. H2 — Pick your era
3. H3 — The edit is the outfit
4. H4 — Five moods, one roll
5. H5 — Your post, on film
