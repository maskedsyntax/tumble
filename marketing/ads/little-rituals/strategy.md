## Exploring (top 3)

- Story: travel from morning coffee to a late-night diner in one compressed day.
- Relatable: make each scene feel like the viewer's own ordinary weekend.
- Product-first: let the scene-specific grades and tactile transitions carry nearly all the copy.

## Not using, and why

- FOMO and urgency: there is no campaign deadline or scarce inventory.
- Social proof: no verified review or adoption evidence is supplied.
- Pure transformation: honest before/after remains the proof device, but it is not the organizing idea because recent ads already use it.

## Selected

Story, supported by relatable and product-first craft. The passage of a single day makes the filter choice feel editorial rather than random.
