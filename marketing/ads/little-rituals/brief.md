# Campaign brief

- Product: Tumble
- Campaign: Little Rituals
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Format: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, vertical
- Channel: Instagram Reels / short-form social
- Forced strategy (if any): none
- Assumptions: original generated lifestyle photos are acceptable source material; the selected after images must come from Tumble's production-equivalent grade renderer.

## Job of this ad

Show that an ordinary day contains several visual moods, then demonstrate why choosing a scene-appropriate film look is more expressive than putting one preset over everything.

## Single-minded proposition

Your day is not one color. Tumble gives each small moment a film character that fits it.

## Proof we can show

- Five honest same-pixel before/after transformations.
- Five scene-specific selections using real looks: Warm Archive, Everyday, Date Stamp, Pool Party, and a second Everyday treatment where the softer grade best preserves the diner portrait.
- The verified library size: 21 film looks.
