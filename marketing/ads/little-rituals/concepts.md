# Concepts

Three different ideas — not three palettes.

## Concept A — A Day in Five Stocks
- Strategy: story + product-first
- Hook: YOUR DAY ISN'T ONE COLOR
- Core idea: move through one ordinary day from dawn to midnight; each ritual receives a film look chosen for its light and feeling.
- Visual metaphor: a cream film edge travels through the day, changing behavior with steam, flower stems, a record sleeve, rain, and flash.
- Emotional intent: attentive, warm, tactile, quietly alive.
- Product demonstration: five same-pixel reveals using real Tumble outputs.
- Pacing: gentle first beat, increasingly precise match cuts, one bright diner climax, confident end-card hold.
- Typography: one large hook, small time/look pairings, one verified library-size claim.
- Transitions: steam rise, diagonal stem wipe, sleeve flip, rainline pull, flash bloom.
- Ending / CTA: the five graded moments fold into a living contact sheet behind the Tumble icon and App Store CTA.
- Why it could work: filter choice becomes part of the story rather than a feature list.
- Asset feasibility: complete; five original generated photos and the full real grade matrix exist.
- Execution risk: the chronological idea can feel like a montage unless each handoff inherits a physical line or light cue from the prior scene.

## Concept B — The Look Chooses You

- Strategy: curiosity + demo
- Hook: PICK THE FEELING
- Core idea: a single animated film edge scans a contact sheet and selects the best-matching look for each photo.
- Visual metaphor: a casting audition for film stocks.
- Emotional intent: playful and taste-driven.
- Product demonstration: rejected looks flicker briefly before the chosen grade locks in.
- Pacing: quick comparisons with satisfying stops.
- Typography: scene names and one-word mood labels.
- Transitions: scanning light and rack-focus movement.
- Ending / CTA: “21 FILM LOOKS” with five chosen frames.
- Why it could work: directly dramatizes selection rather than generic application.
- Asset feasibility: complete.
- Execution risk: showing rejected grades can make the visual argument feel indecisive in 15 seconds.

## Concept C — The Pocket Contact Sheet

- Strategy: relatable + premium
- Hook: THE SMALL STUFF, ON FILM
- Core idea: one physical contact sheet is pulled from a pocket and reveals the entire day as the camera travels across its frames.
- Visual metaphor: a day printed as one tangible artifact.
- Emotional intent: collectible and intimate.
- Product demonstration: each frame develops as the camera reaches it.
- Pacing: one continuous controlled camera move.
- Typography: handwritten time marks only.
- Transitions: uninterrupted macro tracking across paper fibers and frame edges.
- Ending / CTA: pull out to show the sheet beside the Tumble icon.
- Why it could work: closely matches the product's print materiality.
- Asset feasibility: complete.
- Execution risk: one top-down layout could fail the slideshow test if the camera travel is not strongly dimensional.

## Ranking

1. A Day in Five Stocks
2. The Look Chooses You
3. The Pocket Contact Sheet

## Selected

A Day in Five Stocks.

## Shelved for later
