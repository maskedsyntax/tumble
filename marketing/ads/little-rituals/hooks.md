# Hooks

Each hook must be visually executable in ~1.5s and work with sound off.

## Strategy: story

### H1
- Line: YOUR DAY ISN'T ONE COLOR
- Visual: an extreme crop of coffee steam carries a warm develop seam upward while the rest of the kitchen remains cool and raw.
- First frame: the cup, hand, and giant cropped words DAY / COLOR share the frame; the grade is already moving.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 9 / 9 / 10 / 9 / 9 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; expressive campaign language supported by five visibly different scenes.

### H2

- Line: ONE DAY. FIVE FILMS.
- Visual: five narrow time-of-day strips snap open, then one expands into the morning coffee shot.
- First frame: an abstract five-color contact sheet with recognizable fragments of cup, flowers, records, rain, and diner light.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 9 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes as a visual metaphor; five real film looks are shown.

### H3

- Line: PICK THE FEELING
- Visual: the same cream film edge snakes through five photos and stops at the coffee cup as its matching grade appears.
- First frame: a moving film edge bisects a raw and graded macro image.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 9 / 8
- Claim-safe: yes.

### H4

- Line: THE SMALL STUFF, ON FILM
- Visual: crumbs, flower petals, and a record sleeve match-cut at macro scale before the first full print is revealed.
- First frame: an extreme crumb-and-steam texture that does not resemble a generic app ad.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 9 / 9 / 8 / 9 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; “on film” is category language for the film-camera aesthetic, not a claim of physical film stock.

### H5

- Line: DON'T PRESET THE WHOLE DAY
- Visual: one uniform tint tries to cover all five frames, then tears into five scene-specific grades.
- First frame: five unrelated moments trapped under the same muddy color wash.
- Scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty: 8 / 8 / 9 / 8 / 7 / 9
- Claim-safe: yes; a creative direction rather than a superiority claim.
