# Raw source-photo provenance

These five source images were generated with the built-in image generation
tool for this campaign. They intentionally imitate unedited, current-day phone
snapshots: level camera, flat or blunt lighting, casual framing, contemporary
clothes, no vintage grade, no grain, no leaks, and no readable brand marks.

Prompt subjects:

1. Three adult friends around a cluttered apartment table after dinner.
2. Four adult friends beside a hatchback at night under direct phone flash.
3. An older couple and adult daughter blowing out birthday candles in a normal kitchen.
4. Two adult siblings washing an old bicycle on a cloudy driveway.
5. Three adult friends talking at the edge of a modest community pool.

All five were explicitly prompted to avoid editorial light, dramatic angles,
professional styling, shallow depth of field, retro clothing, film effects,
logos, text, and watermarks. The complete production prompts are preserved in
the campaign conversation; this file records the durable constraints and
subjects that affect reuse.
