# Concepts

## 1. The moment becomes an object — selected

A full-bleed ordinary snapshot lifts out of the screen as a physical print. A moving optical wave reveals a real Tumble grade. Four more straight-on prints travel past at different depths, each carrying a separate named look, then lock into a small floating archive around the Tumble mark.

## 2. Film ribbon

An endless clean film strip runs vertically through a scanner gate. Each frame starts raw, crosses the light, and exits with a distinct grade. More graphic and faster, but less emotionally intimate.

## 3. Shoebox gravity

Ordinary raw photos fall weightlessly through a dark room, gain paper borders and grade mid-flight, then settle into a shoebox. Strongest nostalgia metaphor, but risks hiding the honest pixel-for-pixel transformation.

Concept 1 wins: it balances proof, emotion, and smooth dimensional motion while keeping the camera level and the images easy to judge.
