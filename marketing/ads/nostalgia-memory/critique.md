## Slideshow test

Conditional pass. The scanner, circular, horizontal, and water reveals are real motion, and prints enter/leave in depth. However, the resting middle compositions repeat a centered card at similar scale, so v2 should vary framing materially.

## Five highest-impact fixes

1. Give Flash Night, Sepia Print, Overcast, and Pool Party distinct resting scale and x/y placement.
2. Put `KEEP THE ORDINARY.` behind the central archive print so it acts as a depth layer and never blocks faces.
3. Change the end-card category line to `FILM CAMERA FOR IPHONE`, retaining `21 FILM LOOKS` separately.
4. Increase the size of the Pool Party beat so the water reveal feels like a payoff rather than another card.
5. Hold the final category copy fully readable for at least one second.

## v2/final revision result

- Middle prints now use distinct width, resting x/y position, and scale.
- Pool Party is the largest post-hook print and its water-up reveal is easier to read.
- Archive type sits in depth around the central print instead of crossing faces.
- End card now separates `21 FILM LOOKS` from `FILM CAMERA FOR IPHONE` and holds through frame 449.
- Final render passed typecheck, H.264/AAC encode, duration/resolution probe, and first/mid/late/final frame review.

## User-review correction

The archive headline was still too deeply occluded in the reviewed export. It
is now a single line in a dedicated gap between the center and lower prints;
the lower pair moves down to preserve clear space throughout the headline hold.

## Platform-safe correction

During the brand resolve, the complete archive composition now recedes to 86%
and shifts down 95 px. The final hold leaves approximately 250 px above the
top photos and 300 px below the lower photos, protecting the composition from
Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts interface overlays.

## Image-transition correction

The original handoffs briefly cleared the stage before the next print reached
its resting position. The four filter beats now overlap for 19 frames: the
outgoing print carries leftward momentum while the next print enters from the
right, with restrained velocity blur and a warm paper-edge light sweep joining
the movement. The final Pool Party print instead recedes into the assembling
archive. Filter labels exchange vertically so they never collide during the
overlap. A 20-frame transition contact sheet confirmed there are no empty reset
frames between photos.

## Transition-speed correction

The connected handoffs were still reading too quickly in motion. Entry, exit,
paper-edge travel, velocity blur, and label exchange are now retimed together
across approximately 30 frames (one second) per photo change. The overall ad
remains 15 seconds; the extra transition time comes from the static hold inside
each filter beat. A six-stage frame review of every handoff confirmed continuous
occupancy, readable reveals, and the unchanged platform-safe end card.
