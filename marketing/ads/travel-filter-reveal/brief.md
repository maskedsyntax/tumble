# Campaign brief

- Product: Tumble
- Campaign: Travel Filter Reveal
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, silent
- Channel: short-form social / performance
- Forced strategy (if any): transformation, requested before/after sequence
- Assumptions: use five generated source photos because the final explicit count was five; “Available for iPhone” is user-supplied CTA copy.

## Job of this ad

Make Tumble's film-grade delta understandable without explaining the app UI.

## Single-minded proposition

The same travel photo can take on a distinct film character in Tumble.

## Proof we can show

Five generated source photographs paired with output from Tumble's production-equivalent Core Image grade renderer.

## CTA
