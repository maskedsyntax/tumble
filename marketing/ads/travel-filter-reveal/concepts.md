# Concepts

## 1. Traveling prints — selected

Each source photo is a physical print in shallow 3D space. A luminous seam develops it from before to after; the finished print swings forward and the next arrives behind it. Best balance of proof, pace, and tactile Tumble identity.

## 2. Five windows

Travel scenes appear as architectural windows that open from neutral reality into a film memory. More metaphorical, but it weakens the exact same-pixel comparison.

## 3. Contact-sheet runway

A long strip of negatives races vertically while individual frames stop and develop. Strong category cue, but too busy for five readable comparisons in 15 seconds.

Ranking: Traveling prints 9.3, Contact-sheet runway 8.2, Five windows 7.8.
