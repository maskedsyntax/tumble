# Strategy

Scores are the average of product fit, audience fit, hook strength, visual potential, emotional strength, conversion potential, asset feasibility, and originality.

1. Transformation — 9.4. Honest same-pixel before/after is the strongest proof.
2. Product-first — 9.0. The film output is visually desirable enough to carry the ad.
3. Demo — 8.8. A moving grade wipe makes the mechanism instantly legible.
4. Aspirational — 8.0. Travel imagery connects the grade to moments worth keeping.
5. Relatable — 7.5. Familiar travel-photo subjects make the use case immediate.

Chosen direction: transformation led, with product-first materiality and a precise demo wipe. The stills behave like moving prints in depth, not a repeated slide template.
